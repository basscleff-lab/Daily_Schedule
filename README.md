## 🎧 The Floating Hardware Transport Bar (Zero-Friction Daily Use)

The floating transport bar is stripped down to **pure tracking essentials** with zero distractions and **no 2nd action needed**:

1. In this folder (`C:\Apps\Daily_Schedule`), find:
   **`Launch_Floating_Toolbar.bat`**
2. **Right-click** → **Send to → Desktop (create shortcut)**.
3. Name it **"Sabrina Transport Toolbar"**.
4. Every morning she just double-clicks that icon:
   - **AUTO-STARTS IMMEDIATELY**: Opening the app **instantly clocks her in** (`● REC (ON)` with a glowing green LED). She does **not** need to remember to click anything!
   - **Always-On-Top**: Hovers directly over Telus, Outlook, and booking calendars.
   - **Draggable**: Click and drag the top title bar to move it anywhere on her screens.
   - **Simple Shift Controls**:
     - **`PAUSE`** (amber): Tap when taking lunch or pausing between split shifts (changes to amber LED).
     - **`RESUME`**: Tap to resume work.
     - **`STOP`** (red): Tap at the end of a shift segment (e.g. 3 PM or 7 PM) to save her hours.
   - **1-Tap Activity Switching**: Tap `[Inbound Call]`, `[Outbound Call]`, `[Text SMS]`, `[Email]`, or `[Admin Work]` to tag what she is doing.
   - **1-Tap Appointment Counter**: Tap `[+]` next to `APPT:` whenever she books an appointment for Optima Windows & Doors.

## 📄 Preparing Invoices for Optima Windows & Doors

The floating transport bar is kept clean and free of invoice clutter. When it's time to bill her boss at the end of the week or month:
1. Double-click **`Launch_Sabrina_Tracker.bat`** (or open `index.html`).
2. Click the **"Invoice for Optima"** tab.
3. Select the billing date range, check her hourly rate, and click **🖨️ Print / Save as PDF**.
4. It itemizes all her split-shift days, phone vs. off-phone hours, appointments booked, and total amount due.
