# ⏱️ Daily Work Hub & Invoice Tracker
**Lightweight Remote Work Tracker, Appointment Booking & Billable Hours Engine**

---

## 🎧 The Floating Hardware Transport Bar
The floating transport bar is stripped down to **pure tracking essentials** with zero distractions:

1. Double-click **`Launch_Floating_Toolbar.bat`**.
2. **Auto-starts immediately**: Clock-in starts automatically (`● REC (ON)` with green status indicator).
3. **Always-On-Top**: Stays visible over softphones, email, and booking calendars.
4. **Shift Controls**:
   - **`PAUSE`** / **`RESUME`**: Easily manage breaks and split shifts.
   - **`STOP`**: Saves completed shift hours to local storage.
5. **Activity Tags**: Categorize calls, SMS, emails, and off-phone administrative duties.
6. **Appointment & Callback Management**: Quick-book consultations and manage follow-ups.

---

## 📄 Automated Invoicing (15-Minute Upward Rounding)
- Generates clean, billable invoices with daily hours rounded up to the nearest 15-minute increment.
- Instant PDF preview, print, and CSV export.
- Fully customizable contractor and client billing settings stored locally on your machine.

---

## 🛡️ Zero-Data-Loss & Privacy
- All client records, callbacks, shift timestamps, and personal data are stored **strictly in local storage** on your computer.
- Remote updates via Git are configured with strict exclusion rules so work data is never overwritten or transmitted publicly.
